About RIT

ISRO Hackathon 2025 · AI-Powered Climate Digital Twin

v1.0 · Demo Build
Project Overview

RIT is a high-fidelity AI-powered Climate Digital Twin of the Indian subcontinent, developed for the ISRO Hackathon 2026. It integrates satellite remote sensing data from ISRO's INSAT constellation with IMD gridded observations to provide real-time climate monitoring, short-term forecasting, and what-if scenario simulation.

🎯
Purpose
Create an operational climate intelligence platform for India that bridges satellite data, AI models, and actionable climate insights for policymakers and disaster management agencies.
🔬
Digital Twin Concept
A digital twin is a real-time virtual replica of a physical system. For climate, this means a continuously updated AI model of India's atmosphere that mirrors actual conditions and enables "what-if" experimentation without real-world consequences.
🚀
Key Objectives
7-day temperature & rainfall prediction at 0.25° resolution · Real-time extreme event detection · Scenario simulation for climate risk assessment · Scalable architecture for operational deployment.
Key Features
🗺️
Interactive Climate Map
0.25° resolution Leaflet map with temperature and rainfall layers, animated weather effects, and state-level hover information.
📅
7-Day Forecast
Calendar-based forecast view with weather condition icons, city-specific statistics, and interactive date selection.
⚗️
What-If Simulation
Scenario engine with real-time sliders for temperature, rainfall, humidity, and CO₂ with projected impact analysis.
🚨
Alerts & Warnings
Severity-classified weather alerts with actionable Do's and Don'ts, filterable by status and severity level.
📊
Climate Reports
Auto-generated PDF climate reports with AI summaries, weekly trend charts, and per-city analysis.
📈
Historical Analysis
25-year historical timeline scrubber showing temperature trends, rainfall anomalies, and extreme event frequencies.
